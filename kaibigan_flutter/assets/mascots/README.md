# Final Kaibigan mascots

Finalized on October 12, 2026. These are the approved slimmer designs.

- **Kai:** boyish proportions, smaller head, slim body, slate-blue bandana and honeycomb detail.
- **Ann:** matching balanced proportions, peach flower and botanical scarf.

| Pose | Kai | Ann |

| Celebrating | [kai-celebrating.png](kai-celebrating.png) | [ann-celebrating.png](ann-celebrating.png) |

All six files are 1254 × 1254 PNGs with genuine transparent alpha and fully framed characters. The existing `assets/mascots/` declaration includes them in the Flutter asset bundle. `manifest.json` maps each companion and pose to its final asset path.

The chubby Kai asset and its generated source drafts have been deleted. The original Ann image is archived outside the Flutter asset bundle at `output/mascot-archive/ann-bee-v1.png`.

The finalized generation prompts are preserved in [VARIANT_PROMPTS-v3.md](VARIANT_PROMPTS-v3.md). The images were made with the built-in image-generation tool. The AI journal uses the welcoming and listening artwork in its header, replies, waiting state and saved insights.
